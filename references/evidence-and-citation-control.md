# Evidence And Citation Control

## Retrieval

Retrieve external sources when the task requires current, authoritative, or
precise information. Record why the search was needed, what source was
selected, the access or rights basis, the decision supported, and when the
information should be rechecked.

Use official, primary, or otherwise fit-for-purpose sources. Do not treat a
search snippet, an unverified summary, or a citation string as sufficient
evidence.

Use assets/external-evidence-register.template.md.

## Claim Support

For a consequential claim, identify:

- the exact claim and its qualifiers;
- the source identity and version;
- the exact supporting location, such as page, section, table, figure, or
  paragraph;
- a concise support note explaining what the source establishes and what it
  does not establish;
- editorial status and source fitness;
- the reviewer and current disposition.

Use assets/reference-audit.template.md and assets/claim-register.template.csv.

Do not reproduce protected source text unnecessarily. A precise locator and a
short analytical note are usually preferable to a long copied passage.

## Freshness And Limits

Recheck sources when journal requirements, policies, software behavior,
regulations, data-access rules, or other time-sensitive facts may have changed.
Do not extrapolate a source beyond its population, setting, design, outcome, or
stated limitations.
