# Manuscript-Governance Templates

The templates in `assets/manuscript-governance/` are blank generic structures for human-governed manuscript, revision, declaration, submission, and decision review.
They are optional records. A completed template is not a scientific conclusion, author decision, current external requirement, compliance determination, publication approval, or submission authorization.

## Available Templates

| Template | Intended bounded use | Required human control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `manuscript-assembly-claim-display-review.template.md` | Record assembly scope, claim/display relationships, and unresolved items. | Human reviews project evidence, scope, and display accuracy. |
| `reviewer-response-revision-trace.template.md` | Record a bounded revision cycle without copying confidential content. | Human controls confidentiality, response adequacy, and route actions. |
| `declaration-ai-use-fact-boundary.template.md` | Separate known declaration or AI-use facts from unknowns and policy inputs. | Human verifies facts and current policy requirements. |
| `submission-route-package-provenance.template.md` | Record a proposed route and package inventory. | Human verifies current requirements and approves any external action. |
| `decision-ready-human-review-packet.template.md` | Present options, evidence references, and unresolved items for a decision. | The accountable human makes and records the decision. |

## Required Boundary

Use current official sources for a real journal, publisher, institution, funder, reporting guideline, authorship question, AI-use policy, or submission portal.
Do not treat bundled wording as a current external requirement.
Do not put real manuscripts, reviewer correspondence, editorial messages, declarations, submission records, credentials, restricted data, or confidential project facts in the public package.

When a record is revised, re-review the changed record and the directly affected claims, references, and decisions.
Do not carry a prior approval to a changed artifact without an accountable-human decision.
