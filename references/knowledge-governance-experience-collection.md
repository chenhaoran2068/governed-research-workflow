# Knowledge-Governance Experience Collection

The public collection at
`system/06_memory_and_learning/knowledge_governance_experience_collection/`
contains generic, self-contained lessons about governing source and knowledge
work. It is a learning resource, not a `Knowledge` content package, source
library, registry, index, retrieval service, RAG system, or current-source
checker.

Each entry states a scope, lesson, non-claim, and stop boundary. The entries
may help a human ask better questions about admission, freshness,
transformation, retrieval boundaries, maintenance, and expansion. They do not
grant access, verify rights, prove a source current, approve a transformation,
or authorize publication.

Use a public experience entry only after comparing the current task with the
entry's scope. A prior lesson is not automatically reusable when the owner,
material class, authority, environment, or risk has changed. For a real task,
use current authoritative sources and the task's own accountable-human review.

Do not add source payloads, extracted text, transformed notes, curated cards,
retrieval outputs, private records, project facts, local paths, receipts,
hashes, traces, or historical approval evidence to this public collection.
