# Historical KGE Public Collection Entry Point

`KGE-001` through `KGE-038` are historical public identifiers from the v0.11
collection. For the current public entrypoint, use
`references/public-safe-shared-experience-derivatives.md` and the public
vocabulary, catalogue, and cards under `assets/public-experience-derivatives/`.

The v1.6 cards retain only a public backward reference to the equal-numbered
KGE identifier. They are generic guidance and retrieval navigation only, not a
Knowledge content package, source library, RAG capability, current-source
checker, approval, promotion mechanism, or authority to act. Older immutable
Releases preserve the historical KGE collection without being rewritten here.
