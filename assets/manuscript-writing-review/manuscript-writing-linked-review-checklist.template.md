## 1. Narrative Weight And Results Role

- [ ] Does the structure make the central question, main result, and principal
      interpretation easy to find?
- [ ] Do section and paragraph lengths follow scientific work rather than a
      mechanically uniform pattern?
- [ ] Are background and routine procedures proportionate to consequential
      design choices and main findings?
- [ ] Are the main result, key secondary findings, and material sensitivity or
      exploratory findings distinguishable by role?
- [ ] Are prespecified primary and key secondary outcomes reported whether or
      not they are statistically prominent?
- [ ] Does each Results paragraph have one reporting job?

## 2. Information Placement And Limitations

- [ ] Are reproducibility-critical definitions, time windows, algorithms, and
      missing-data methods in Methods or Supplementary Methods?
- [ ] Are realised counts, denominators, estimates, and observed missingness in
      Results and the linked display?
- [ ] Can each display be read from its own title, labels, legend, and note
      without guessing its units, denominator, or scale direction?
- [ ] Are material study-wide limitations together in a coherent Discussion
      location?
- [ ] Are result-specific qualifications kept next to the result only when they
      change its meaning?
- [ ] Do the abstract and conclusion retain limitations that materially change
      their scope?

## 3. Eligibility And Flow, If Applicable

- [ ] Is the selection unit stated?
- [ ] Are inclusion and exclusion rules distinct from implementation details?
- [ ] Do Methods, code outputs, Results, displays, and table notes use the same
      selection logic and denominators?
- [ ] Does a needed cohort-flow display show real operational steps and distinct
      analysis-specific populations?
- [ ] Are displayed branch or exclusion counts mutually exclusive, or clearly
      labelled when they overlap?
- [ ] Are both editable and rendered display forms identified and reviewed?

## 5. Candidate Reconciliation

- [ ] Are the source manuscript, editable displays, rendered package, result
      package, QA record, references, declarations, and supplements identified?
- [ ] Have title, abstract, objective, population, flow, main estimates,
      displays, references, declarations, and supplements been reconciled?
- [ ] After each bounded revision, were directly affected text, displays,
      citations, translations, and rendered pages re-reviewed?
- [ ] Are unresolved author, ethics, access, funding, conflict, journal, or
      approval facts visible instead of inferred?
- [ ] Is the package still labelled as a candidate until accountable human
      decisions establish the applicable scientific and submission authority?
