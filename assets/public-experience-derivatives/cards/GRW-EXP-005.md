# Responsible Ownership Before Collection

## Applicable Context

Reviewing ownership of a source-collection process.

## Generic Experience

Identify the responsible human and the permitted transformation boundary before collection.

## Recommended Behavior

Name the accountable owner and the transformation limit before work begins.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Does not establish institutional permission. Stop when ownership or duty is unclear.
