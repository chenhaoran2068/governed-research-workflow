# Task-Specific Current-Source Verification

## Applicable Context

Verifying an asserted current-source condition.

## Generic Experience

Tie any current-source statement to a dated, attributable, task-specific verification record.

## Recommended Behavior

State who checked what condition and when for the current task.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Does not turn a past check into a standing guarantee. Stop when evidence is absent, stale, or outside scope.
