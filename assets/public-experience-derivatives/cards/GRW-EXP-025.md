# Object-Specific Derivative Review

## Applicable Context

Piloting a cleaned derivative for another object.

## Generic Experience

Record object-specific limitations and review needs even when a template is reused.

## Recommended Behavior

Make material differences visible before applying a reused template.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A reusable template is not automatic approval. Stop when reuse hides a material difference.
