# Visible Repair and Downstream Re-Review

## Applicable Context

Repairing a stale-event representation.

## Generic Experience

Record what changed, why it changed, and which downstream items require re-review.

## Recommended Behavior

Preserve historical evidence and name affected downstream material before closure.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A repair record does not prove all downstream effects are resolved. Stop when a correction would silently rewrite historical evidence.
