# Visible Plan State and Controlled Plan Change

## Applicable Context

A declared multi-step task with meaningful scope, order, or stop boundaries.

## Generic Experience

Progress updates are different from changing the task plan itself.

## Recommended Behavior

Keep the current plan visible, explain the reason and impact of a material
plan change, and retain an accountable-human decision point before replacing
the plan.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A visible plan is not authority to act and does not require a fixed interface.
Stop for a safety, permission, or boundary uncertainty even when the previous
plan would continue.
