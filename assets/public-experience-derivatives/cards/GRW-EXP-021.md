# Retrieval Refusal as Designed Behavior

## Applicable Context

Hardening a retrieval refusal boundary.

## Generic Experience

Treat path, access, scope, and unknown-condition refusals as designed behavior.

## Recommended Behavior

Test refusal paths and verify that they neither disclose nor alter material.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A refusal check does not prove that permitted retrieval is correct. Stop when an error path could disclose or alter material.
