# Declared Rebuild and Evaluation Limits

## Applicable Context

Preparing rebuild and stable-evaluation work.

## Generic Experience

Define reproducibility inputs, identity boundaries, and evaluation limits before rerunning a process.

## Recommended Behavior

Check that required inputs and evaluation criteria are explicit before a rebuild.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A rebuild plan does not prove reproducibility. Stop when required inputs or evaluation criteria are missing.
