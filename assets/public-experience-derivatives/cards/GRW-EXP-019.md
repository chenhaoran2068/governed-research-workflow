# Curated Card With Visible Uncertainty

## Applicable Context

Running a small curated-card pilot.

## Generic Experience

Keep the summary form, provenance, and human interpretation visibly separate.

## Recommended Behavior

Preserve uncertainty and distinguish summary form from interpretation.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A card does not prove semantic correctness. Stop when a summary loses its source boundary or uncertainty.
