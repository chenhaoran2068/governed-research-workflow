# Derivation Needs Object-Specific Readiness

## Applicable Context

Planning derived expansion from a ready object.

## Generic Experience

Require object-specific readiness evidence before deriving additional representations.

## Recommended Behavior

Reassess rights, sensitivity, and intended use for every next derivative.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Readiness for one derivation does not authorize another. Stop when the next derivation changes rights, sensitivity, or intended use.
