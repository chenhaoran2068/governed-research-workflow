# Evidence Before Pilot Expansion

## Applicable Context

Deciding whether a pilot may expand.

## Generic Experience

Require explicit evidence, unresolved-risk review, and a human decision before expansion.

## Recommended Behavior

Check each prerequisite and record disputed or unverified conditions before widening scope.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Passing earlier checks is not expansion authorization. Stop when any prerequisite is unverified or disputed.
