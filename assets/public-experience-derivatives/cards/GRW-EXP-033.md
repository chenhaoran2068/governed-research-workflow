# Rebuild the Baseline Before Reuse

## Applicable Context

Rebuilding a baseline before reuse.

## Generic Experience

Re-establish the current identity and assumptions before relying on prior outputs.

## Recommended Behavior

Compare the earlier baseline with the present task before carrying forward a conclusion.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A baseline rebuild is not a release or compliance approval. Stop when the earlier baseline cannot be compared honestly.
