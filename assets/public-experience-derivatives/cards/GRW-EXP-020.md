# Narrow Retrieval Baseline

## Applicable Context

Establishing a narrow retrieval baseline.

## Generic Experience

Use a limited baseline to test declared behavior before widening scope.

## Recommended Behavior

Check declared inputs, outputs, and refusal behavior before considering any expansion.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A baseline is not a full service or support guarantee. Stop when a result would require unapproved source expansion.
