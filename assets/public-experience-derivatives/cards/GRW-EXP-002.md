# Risk-Separated Governance Roadmap

## Applicable Context

Planning a staged source-governance roadmap.

## Generic Experience

Separate phases by risk and evidence rather than treating a roadmap as permission.

## Recommended Behavior

Give each next phase its own scope, evidence, and human decision.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Does not make later phases approved. Stop when the next phase lacks its own scope or human decision.
