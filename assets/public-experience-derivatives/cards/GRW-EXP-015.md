# Accountable Curated Derivative Card

## Applicable Context

Creating a curated derivative card.

## Generic Experience

State the intended use, limitations, and accountable human for any curated summary.

## Recommended Behavior

Keep the summary purpose and human review boundary visible to a reader.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A curated card is not a complete evidence base or current requirement. Stop when summary language would overstate source support.
