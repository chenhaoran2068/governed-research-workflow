# Separate Active and Legacy Routes

## Applicable Context

Separating active and legacy routes.

## Generic Experience

Label current and historical routes distinctly so older material is not silently reused.

## Recommended Behavior

State route identity and applicability before relying on older material.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A legacy route is not invalid solely because it is old. Stop when route identity or current applicability is unknown.
