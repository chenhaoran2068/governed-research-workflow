# Dated Freshness Assessment

## Applicable Context

Reviewing metadata-only freshness fields.

## Generic Experience

Record the basis, date, and uncertainty of a freshness assessment separately from source content.

## Recommended Behavior

Preserve the assessment basis and its review date as metadata.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Metadata structure does not prove currentness. Stop when a freshness conclusion would require unperformed retrieval.
