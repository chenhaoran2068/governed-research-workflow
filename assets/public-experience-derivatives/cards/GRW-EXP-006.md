# Metadata Registration Without Content Copying

## Applicable Context

Registering a source without copying its content.

## Generic Experience

Prefer a bounded pointer or metadata representation when content access is unnecessary.

## Recommended Behavior

Keep the representation limited to the minimum necessary descriptive fields.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A pointer does not prove that a target is current, accessible, or suitable. Stop when registration would disclose restricted locator details.
