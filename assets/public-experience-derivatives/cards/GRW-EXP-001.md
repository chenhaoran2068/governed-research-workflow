# Bounded Metadata-Only Planning

## Applicable Context

Early planning for a small source-admission exercise.

## Generic Experience

Start with a bounded description and a human decision record before handling content.

## Recommended Behavior

Record known scope, unknowns, and the intended boundary before any content action.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Does not prove that any source may be obtained or used. Stop when ownership, access, or intended use is unknown.
