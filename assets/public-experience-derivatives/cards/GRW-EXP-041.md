# Governed Task Initiation and Human Gates

## Applicable Context

Starting a consequential or multi-step task under human governance.

## Generic Experience

A useful task plan identifies its intended action, purpose, basis, expected
output, cautions, stop condition, and accountable-human decision point before
substantive work begins.

## Recommended Behavior

State known facts and unknowns, identify the next bounded action, and stop for
the required human decision instead of filling an authorization or evidence
gap with assumptions.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

This guidance is not a task executor, delegation mechanism, approval record,
or standing permission. Stop when scope, inputs, authority, or a material
boundary is unknown or conflicting.
