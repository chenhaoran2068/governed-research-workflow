# Minimal Raw-Handling Pilot

## Applicable Context

Testing a narrowly bounded raw-source handling path.

## Generic Experience

Use the smallest authorized pilot and preserve the distinction between source and derivative.

## Recommended Behavior

Limit retained material and make the transformation boundary explicit.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A small pilot does not authorize broader copying. Stop when work would retain or expose content beyond its approved boundary.
