# Accountable Interpretation Boundary

## Applicable Context

Keeping interpretation with its accountable owner.

## Generic Experience

Distinguish a source-derived representation from a human conclusion or recommendation.

## Recommended Behavior

Name interpretation review status separately from the derivative representation.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A recorded interpretation is not an approved decision. Stop when ownership, evidence, or review status is missing.
