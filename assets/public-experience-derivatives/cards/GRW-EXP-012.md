# Stabilize a Source Family Before Expansion

## Applicable Context

Stabilizing a small source family.

## Generic Experience

Resolve repeated classification and ownership questions before adding additional families.

## Recommended Behavior

Document unresolved rights, format, and risk differences before broadening coverage.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Local stability does not prove general support. Stop when a new family changes rights, format, or risk.
