# Multi-Object Proof Before Generalization

## Applicable Context

Evaluating a multi-object retrieval proof.

## Generic Experience

Demonstrate declared relationships and refusal behavior before claiming broader operation.

## Recommended Behavior

Limit conclusions to the tested relationships and fixture conditions.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A multi-object test is not evidence of complete coverage or safe scaling. Stop when results are generalized beyond fixture scope.
