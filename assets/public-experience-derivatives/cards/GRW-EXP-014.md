# Bounded Cleaned Derivative Note

## Applicable Context

Creating a cleaned derivative note.

## Generic Experience

Label the transformation purpose, source boundary, and unresolved issues before reuse.

## Recommended Behavior

Preserve a concise statement of purpose, limitations, and open questions with the derivative.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A cleaned note is not the original source and is not automatically public-safe. Stop when a derivative would include restricted or unidentified material.
