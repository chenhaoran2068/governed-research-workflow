# Stale Metadata Needs Re-Review

## Applicable Context

Updating stale index-related information.

## Generic Experience

Treat stale metadata as a maintenance finding with a recorded review need.

## Recommended Behavior

Preserve the earlier state and document why an update is being considered.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A stale marker does not identify the correct replacement. Stop when an update would overwrite evidence without re-review.
