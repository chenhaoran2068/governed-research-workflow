# Stabilize the Core Bundle

## Applicable Context

Stabilizing a core bundle before broader work.

## Generic Experience

Resolve known defects and maintainability issues before adding new scope.

## Recommended Behavior

Close or explicitly hold known control issues before extending a bundle.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Stabilization does not guarantee future compatibility. Stop when scope growth bypasses unresolved controls.
