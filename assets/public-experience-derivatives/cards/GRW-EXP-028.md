# Visible Expansion Readiness Conditions

## Applicable Context

Reviewing conditions before expansion.

## Generic Experience

Use a visible readiness checklist for scope, rights, evidence, and maintenance capacity.

## Recommended Behavior

Give every expansion condition a clear known, unknown, or unmet status.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Readiness review does not itself expand authority. Stop when a required condition is unknown or unmet.
