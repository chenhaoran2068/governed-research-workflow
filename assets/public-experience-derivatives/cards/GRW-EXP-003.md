# Candidate Source Inventory Before Selection

## Applicable Context

Inventorying candidate source records before admission.

## Generic Experience

Record what is known, unknown, and out of scope before selecting a pilot.

## Recommended Behavior

Keep inventory fields bounded and distinguish unknown coverage from confirmed facts.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Does not establish coverage or completeness. Stop when an inventory would require unapproved discovery or content access.
