# Substantive Content Before a Supporting-Material Reference

## Applicable Context

Reporting a result, review finding, change, or handoff that refers to
supporting material.

## Generic Experience

A file location or link does not by itself communicate the task-relevant
result or impact.

## Recommended Behavior

State the relevant finding, change, or conclusion first at a depth
proportionate to importance, then cite the supporting material for
verification or detail.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Navigation-only requests may receive location-only answers. This guidance does
not establish truth, authority, permission, or a right to disclose restricted
material; stop when adequate context would reveal protected content.
