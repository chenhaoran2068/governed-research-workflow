# Expansion Readiness Matrix

## Applicable Context

Assessing expansion readiness with a matrix.

## Generic Experience

Make evidence, unknowns, dependencies, and stop conditions visible for each expansion item.

## Recommended Behavior

Require a visible human disposition for every proposed expansion item.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A matrix is not a decision substitute. Stop when a proposed expansion lacks a human disposition.
