# Declared Retrieval Boundary

## Applicable Context

Designing a bounded retrieval activity.

## Generic Experience

Declare inputs, permitted outputs, stop conditions, and excluded actions before retrieval.

## Recommended Behavior

Require explicit review of rights, tool authority, and output boundaries before beginning.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A retrieval plan does not authorize network, credential, or source-content access. Stop when input rights, tool authority, or output boundary is unknown.
