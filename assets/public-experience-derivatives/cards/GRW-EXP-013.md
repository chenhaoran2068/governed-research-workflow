# Separate Repeatable Capture From Interpretation

## Applicable Context

Separating reproducible extraction from later interpretation.

## Generic Experience

Preserve a clear boundary between repeatable capture steps and human interpretation.

## Recommended Behavior

Describe mechanical inputs and outputs separately from conclusions or recommendations.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Repeatability does not establish meaning or authority. Stop when interpretation is represented as mechanical extraction.
