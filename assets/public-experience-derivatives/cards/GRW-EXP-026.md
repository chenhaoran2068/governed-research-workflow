# Source-Specific Curatorial Uncertainty

## Applicable Context

Piloting a curated derivative for another object.

## Generic Experience

Preserve source-specific uncertainty and a human review boundary.

## Recommended Behavior

Keep accountable ownership visible for every new curatorial decision.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A new card does not establish comparable evidence. Stop when the curatorial decision lacks accountable ownership.
