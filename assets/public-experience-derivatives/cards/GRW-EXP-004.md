# Explicit Source Admission Decision

## Applicable Context

Deciding whether a source object may enter a controlled workflow.

## Generic Experience

Treat source admission as a distinct human decision with explicit boundary evidence.

## Recommended Behavior

Check identity, rights, restrictions, and permitted use before admitting an object.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Does not grant source access or redistribution rights. Stop when rights, restrictions, or object identity are uncertain.
