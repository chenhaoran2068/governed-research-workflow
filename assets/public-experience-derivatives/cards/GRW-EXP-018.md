# Minimal Transformation Pilot

## Applicable Context

Running a small cleaned-note pilot.

## Generic Experience

Test a transformation boundary with minimal material and explicit review points.

## Recommended Behavior

Keep the pilot purpose, boundaries, and review points visible throughout the exercise.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

A pilot outcome is not universal evidence. Stop when the pilot would become a production workflow without review.
