# Bounded Governance Exercise Closure

## Applicable Context

Closing a bounded governance exercise.

## Generic Experience

Record what was learned, what remains unknown, and what requires a later human decision.

## Recommended Behavior

Make unresolved material risks and later decision points visible at closure.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Closure of an exercise is not a standing permission or production claim. Stop when closure would conceal unresolved material risk.
