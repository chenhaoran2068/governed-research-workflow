# Explicit Currentness Questions

## Applicable Context

Planning coverage over time.

## Generic Experience

Represent time boundaries and freshness questions explicitly rather than silently assuming recency.

## Recommended Behavior

State the date basis and uncertainty whenever a task depends on currentness.

## Exclusions And Stop Conditions

Does not make a source current or complete. Stop when a currentness claim lacks a current authorized check.
