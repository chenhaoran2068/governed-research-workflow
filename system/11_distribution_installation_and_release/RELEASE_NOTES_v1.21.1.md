# Governed Research Workflow v1.21.1

## Changed

- Adds an optional documentation-only convention for a new local Study root:
  `Research<number>-<lowercase-hyphenated-slug>`.
- Clarifies that the root name, a separately recorded `study_id`, a
  `study_title`, and a Framework `research_program_id` have distinct roles.
- Preserves existing Study roots as caller-supplied historical locations;
  they are not renamed or migrated.

## Not Included

This release does not choose a number, discover a Study, scan folders, create,
rename, move, or migrate a root. It adds no capability identifier, data access,
external action, research executor, or authority transfer.
