# V1.21 Manuscript Writing And Linked Review Candidate

## Candidate Scope

This local source candidate is based on the committed v1.20.0 state
reconciliation candidate. It adds generic manuscript-writing and linked-review
guidance, one blank checklist, and targeted cross-references to existing
Results-first and joint-review guidance.

The candidate expresses eight reusable practices without project material:

1. narrative weight follows scientific work;
2. limitations are candid without mechanical repetition;
3. information appears where the reader needs it;
4. eligibility separates study rules from implementation detail;
5. cohort flow is a reconciled evidence display;
6. material claims are reviewed with immediate evidence;
7. candidate manuscripts require linked final QA; and
8. Results are structured by result role, not significance alone.

## Public And Authority Boundary

The source contains no Study path, patient-level data, result, manuscript,
author, target-journal decision, correspondence, credential, or private
evidence record. It does not package AMA or other licensed style-manual
content. It does not select a reporting guideline, select a journal, write or
approve a manuscript, validate scientific facts, or authorize submission.

The new checklist is blank generic structure. It records supplied references
and unresolved items but does not read them. Current external instructions,
statistical review, source verification, and accountable-human decisions remain
separate requirements.
