# Plan-State Visibility And Controlled Change

## Purpose

For a declared multi-step task, make the current top-level plan visible as the
work proceeds. Distinguish ordinary step completion from a material change to
the task plan.

## Required Behavior

1. At the start of a declared multi-step task and when entering a substantive
   step, show the current plan with completed, in-progress, and pending work.
2. Normal completion or progress-state updates do not by themselves change
   the structure of the plan.
3. Before changing the plan's structure, order, scope, authorization point,
   completion criterion, or stop condition, state the proposed change, why it
   is needed, and its practical effect.
4. A material plan change requires an accountable-human acceptance before the
   displayed plan is replaced.
5. A safety, permission, or boundary uncertainty requires an immediate stop
   and report even when the existing plan would otherwise continue.

## Boundaries

This guidance does not require a fixed number of steps or a particular user
interface. A visible plan is not authorization to act, does not replace a
separate collaboration or release decision, and does not make a task outcome
correct. Stop for accountable-human review when a proposed change affects a
material boundary or when the required decision is missing, conflicting, or
unknown.
